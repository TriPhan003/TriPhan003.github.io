About me

Tri Phan

I am an aerospace engineering student at Georgia Tech pursuing a career in rocket propulsion. I began at Georgia State University on the pre-mechanical engineering pathway, completed my associate degree with a 4.0 GPA, and transferred to Georgia Tech to study aerospace engineering.

My first research experience was in computational chemistry, studying the excited-state behavior of eumelanin, which built a habit of learning simulation tools quickly and verifying results before relying on them. I now focus on propulsion: I lead the propulsion team on the Georgia Tech Propulsive Lander and am independently evaluating regenerative cooling for a 3 kN LOX/ethanol thrust chamber.
